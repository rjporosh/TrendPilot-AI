# TrendPilot AI — Roadmap

## Phase 0: Discovery

* Identify the actual AI/token provider.
* Verify API access, model names, rate limits and billing.
* Select visual-generation and Bengali TTS providers.
* Check platform-account eligibility and permissions.

## Phase 1: Foundation

* Python project and configuration.
* SQLite database and migrations.
* Logging, job tracking and mock adapters.
* Health checks and cost ledger.

## Phase 2: Video Pipeline

* Topic selection and original script generation.
* Storyboard, visual assets and Bengali narration.
* Captions and FFmpeg rendering.
* Output validation and asset manifest.

## Phase 3: Safety Dashboard

* Rights and license records.
* Factuality, quality and disclosure checks.
* Video preview and approval.
* Audit log and emergency stop.

## Phase 4: YouTube

* OAuth and permissions.
* Test upload and processing-status checks.
* Quota handling and duplicate prevention.

## Phase 5: Meta

* Official Facebook and Instagram authorization.
* Page/account eligibility checks.
* Reels upload and publication workflow.
* Error handling and remote post tracking.

## Phase 6: Analytics

* Available metrics collection.
* Time-series snapshots.
* Niche and content-performance comparison.
* Cost-versus-performance reporting.

## Phase 7: Bounded Automation

* Daily scheduling and upload caps.
* Budget limits and retry queues.
* Per-platform auto-publish settings.
* Controlled burn-in before unattended operation.

## Phase 8: Deployment

* Optional cloud hosting and object storage.
* Secure secret management.
* Backups, monitoring and recovery testing.

### Suggested Initial Timeline

Week 1: foundation and provider verification.
Week 2: video generation.
Week 3: safety dashboard and YouTube tests.
Week 4: Meta integration and analytics foundation.

This is an estimate, not a delivery guarantee. External API access and account approvals may extend the timeline.
